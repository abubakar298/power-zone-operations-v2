# Milestone 5 — Commercial pricing and quotation versions

An approved BOQ supports Create quotation, which opens commercial pricing with that exact revision selected. Pending review offers Approve & save and Approve & create quotation. Both approve the same preserved costing; the latter proceeds directly to pricing. Creating a quotation later from a saved BOQ remains available. Admin/Management prepare commercial pricing and save quotations; other authenticated staff can read/download saved customer quotations. This permissions choice follows the executive pricing workflow; no employee may bypass costing approval.

Pricing uses decimal markup on cost. No Breakup applies BESS and Everything Else percentages; Basic Breakup applies separate Solar, Structure, BESS, Earthing Bores and Balance percentages. Balance is the exact remainder, avoiding double counting. Starting inputs use the handover/reference: No Breakup 15% BESS and 20% rest; Basic 5% Solar, 7% Structure, 33% BESS, 5% Earthing, 25% Balance. All are editable commercial inputs.

The reference's Selling After Tax is used in both presentation modes. Solar tax is 10%, other items 18%, calculated after markup. Tax buckets are rounded to two decimals once with decimal half-up rounding, then discount is deducted. Tax Included uses only Total / Special Discount / Net Total on the customer quotation; no GST, sales-tax labels or tax-mode text appears. Separate-tax presentation uses the exact locked solar/other tax labels. Combined displayed category amounts reconcile to the displayed total; residual penny rounding is carried by Balance.

Customer quotation wording follows the workbook Quotation Print reference, including brand within description, panel count/wattage, inverter quantity/capacity, battery modules/BMS and combined commercial protection/accessory rows. Detailed BOQ specifications and internal costs/markup never populate the customer snapshot. Descriptions, quoted date, validity and proposed-by are editable before saving; notes and terms are the exact locked text. Basic Breakup uses one amount spanning BESS rows and one spanning Balance/EPC rows. No Breakup has no Amount column on scope rows.

Saved pricing is CP-{BOQ number}-Vn; quotation identity is Q-{BOQ number}-Vn. These are deterministic application identifiers retaining the exact BOQ revision. Pricing/quotation request UUIDs provide idempotency; reuse with changed inputs is rejected. Saving appends immutable PostgreSQL snapshots and audit entries. A description/date/validity change creates a new quotation version; changing markup creates a new pricing version. Earlier PDF exports use their original customer snapshot and are not recalculated from current masters.

PDF exports are authenticated server attachments. All four presentation combinations are tested and representative PDFs rendered page by page; totals remain together, fixed import/withholding note and all terms are present. The locked BAFL reference No Breakup 15% BESS / 20% rest yields subtotal 7,601,457.40; solar tax 181,116.00; other tax 1,042,253.53; net 8,824,826.93 before discount, or 8,500,000.00 after 324,826.93 discount.

No customer approval, PO, IPO, inventory, invoice, email-provider activation or new paid service is included in this milestone. User production BOQ and rates are not modified by verification.


Owner-requested quotation refinements: the preview renders the generated PDF pages, with supplied Power Zone logo/contact footer and top-right Confidential label. Quoted date, proposed by and validity align left; contact person/phone are read from customer master and frozen per quotation (blank when absent). Discount may be overridden on a new quotation without altering its pricing version; net total updates in preview and server validation/fingerprint confirmation is required before save. BESS and Balance amount cells span their scope rows without combined labels.

BMS wording is persisted by migration 009 as an appended approved specification and quotation description default. New quotation scope adds the selected battery brand to the IP65 Battery Control and Safety Unit wording. Discount editing overlays the actual PDF Special Discount amount; its Net Total row recalculates locally with Decimal and server confirmation is required before saving. Saved PDFs remain generated from the immutable server snapshot.
