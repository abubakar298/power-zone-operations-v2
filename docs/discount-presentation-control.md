# Discount presentation control

Approved 24 October 2026: “Add discount to selling price” belongs beside Discount (%) in Quotation Settings, for new quotations and revisions. It is unchecked by default. Previewing and saving carries the selected treatment into the quotation and its six download formats. Returning to settings preserves the choice.

The existing allocation excludes solar panels, structure and earthing; approved plus special discount is spread proportionally across eligible selling prices, GST is recalculated and separate customer discount lines are omitted. Discount authorisation and Management approval rules remain unchanged. Unchecked quotes retain standard selling prices and separate discount lines. Existing saved documents are not rewritten.
