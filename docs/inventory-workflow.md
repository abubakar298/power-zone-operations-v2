# Inventory workspace — approved workflow and first implementation

Inventory has four destinations: My Workspace, Stock & Warehouses, Delivery Orders, and Receipts & Reconciliation. Six presentation baskets organize daily work: BOQs Received; Stock & Procurement Actions; Deliveries Due; Dispatch Confirmation Pending; Gate-In & Returns; IRR Received.

## Working first milestone

Existing Operations BOQ handoffs remain reachable through exact preparation records. Job search includes active UBL saved baselines and issued regular IPOs. Operations can attach an installation report to IRR Received. Inventory can also find a job directly, enter dates and material usage, save drafts, attach evidence, review a reconciliation and send it to Accounts. Accounts can retrieve the frozen submission through authorized endpoints; its new workspace is deferred for discussion.

Quantities, dates and evidence are checked before submission. Every save adds an immutable version; stale edits are rejected. Submitted entries and attachments are read-only. Attachment downloads require an active authorized departmental account. Buying costs are not included in Inventory projections.

CHP-320 uses the confirmed 44 PKR/W panel selling reference and original proposal structure, installation and cable references. Combined legacy DC usage is preserved without inventing an actual red/black split. Other jobs whose selling adjustment rates have not been confirmed show unresolved rates, not zero rates. Reconciliation is a potential invoice adjustment only; approved selling totals never change automatically.

## Subsequent stock and dispatch milestone

Operations planning hands off a BOQ. Inventory checks physical, reserved and available stock by warehouse; reserves or requests procurement; schedules or urgently dispatches. Partial deliveries and multiple warehouse DOs stay linked to one job. Actual brands, specifications and panel wattages are recorded. Physical stock deduction requires the signed gatekeeper copy and dispatch confirmation. Transfers remain separate warehouse movements with receiving confirmation. Purchase Gate-In requires vendor delivery evidence and bills; expected site returns do not become stock until physically received.

Stock ledger, reservations, procurement requests, DO issuance and warehouse movement controls are not part of this first deployment. Empty baskets must not invent balances or pending records. Existing preparation checkpoints do not post physical stock movements.

## Documents and Accounts deferred

Printed DO, Material Issuance/Return and Commissioning Acknowledgment layouts await the next review. Accounts IPO approval, return for revision, PT actual costs, closure summaries and Management closure approval are the next separately discussed milestone. Invoicing remains deferred. Job closure never follows from an Inventory submission alone.
