# UBL configurations — phase 1

Four customer-specific hybrid configurations are available from Customers & Sites → UBL configurations. This does not introduce a project, approval or billing workflow.

The four original R3 PDFs are preserved byte-for-byte with SHA-256 checksums. Original technical scope, later site amendments (date unspecified), and twelve supplied commercial negotiation records are immutable and separately visible. The confirmed 10 July 2026 selling prices take precedence over proposal-stage totals.

Management creates the first dated budget and explicitly refreshes subsequent versions. Merely opening the page never refreshes costs. Every saved budget identifies its Rate Master versions and technical version. Panel reference budgets retain 10% markup and full calculation precision; current buying estimates are separate. Actual procurement results remain unavailable until actual costs exist.

The remaining-items calculator proportionally scales monetary markup, preserves costs, handles mixed GST, and displays rounding differences. Missing rates, zero markup pools, below-cost totals and deferred internet are flagged; they never silently produce fabricated budgets or allocations.

Review required: exact purchasing-rate mappings for the DC/AC bundled accessories and enclosures; red/black cable split; a verified single 30 kW inverter purchasing rate; and the internet allowance's eventual commercial allocation. These incomplete inputs prevent a complete reconciled template budget. Known costs remain visible, clearly labelled partial.

Coordinator responses omit BESS purchasing costs and aggregate full-budget/profit figures. Historical summary budgets and refresh notes are restricted to Management/Administrator. Original customer selling proposals remain accessible. Budget creation requires Management/Administrator, active accounts and CSRF. Concurrent refreshes return a reload conflict rather than overwriting prior versions.

No Item Master rates, existing quotes, projects, approvals, or original source documents are changed by this feature.
