# Revision routing

Every further quotation on an already quoted BOQ begins with a revision request. Record the request origin (Internal management or Customer), named requester, reason, authenticated initiator and whether costing/technical scope changes. These are separate classifications: a customer or management request may take either route. Requests and their completed quotation links are immutable and idempotent.

Commercial revisions keep the original approved BOQ. Margin, tax presentation/category, discount, dates and validity may change; the server rejects technical description changes, added/removed quotation-only items, or changed additional-item cost. Quantities and units are always generated from approved costing. Presentation changes may reveal zero-quantity rows but cannot alter their scope. Existing technical descriptions are retained across presentations. A new pricing/quotation snapshot is appended, never written over the source. Only one quotation can complete a request; further changes start a new request.

Costing revisions create an owned survey/BOQ revision draft against the latest approved revision in the same BOQ chain. The source quotation remains linked to its historical BOQ. Survey quantity/brand changes, revision-specific technical specifications, and an optional additional costing item are reviewed through the existing BOQ submission/approval flow. Technical changes require this route even when the amount is unchanged. The approved snapshot and origin are preserved. Revised quotation pricing must reference the exact BOQ revision approved from that request's draft; an unrelated or old approved BOQ is rejected. A quotation-only additional item on an older source is carried into the costing draft, so its cost is included on the revised BOQ.

The optional costing item stores description, specification, quantity, unit, unit cost and tax category in the BOQ snapshot. Its cost contributes once to EPC/Installation and total costing. Pricing excludes that cost from Balance before applying its own editable markup/tax category; quotation Full Breakup shows it as row 17, Basic shows a separate amount, and No Breakup includes scope and the total. Customer snapshots contain selling amounts only. Technical specifications remain BOQ/costing data according to the handover.

Revision requests are listed with pending, costing-approved or completed state and can be resumed. Employees can create and prepare costing revisions; existing commercial permissions remain Admin/Management pending the owner's role-policy decision. No email-provider activation, customer acceptance, purchasing or inventory scope is added. Historical records are not relabeled or recalculated.
