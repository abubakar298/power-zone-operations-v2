# IPO and Peachtree accounts pack

The standard IPO download is one PDF: the IPO first, followed by its Peachtree division. Draft and pending-submission previews use the same combined layout. Accounts and Operations link to this combined document. Previous standalone Peachtree URLs remain compatible, but are no longer separate workflow download buttons.

Quotation type includes presentation (No Breakup, Basic Breakup or Full Breakup) and tax treatment (Tax Included or Show Sales Tax Separately). It appears on both PDF sections, IPO preparation/review/saved records, Operations receipts and issuance documents, and Inventory/Accounts records.

Metadata comes from the exact accepted quotation's immutable pricing version. New Operations and Store snapshots carry it forward; older snapshots are enriched only in read responses using their original IPO/quotation links. Stored historical snapshots are not rewritten. Existing approved discounts, original margins, tax treatment, phase division and payable amounts remain unchanged.

Verification: six presentation/tax combinations preserve payable and identify the same IPO in both sections; legacy division derivation and downstream metadata are checked against immutable records. Browser regression verifies downloading one PDF containing both sections. The sample combined report is visually checked on A4 pages.
