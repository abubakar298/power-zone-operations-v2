# Coordination stages and turnaround

The dashboard derives work buckets from persisted survey, costing, pricing, quotation, IPO and Operations records. Clicking a stage filters its documents; Open workflow selects the specific source document where supported. Management decisions remain restricted to Admin/Management.

Quotation review and sale receipt use append-only coordination events. Submit, management approve/request revision and PO/approval receipt have server timestamps. Requested changes require a new quotation revision; historical quotations remain immutable. Receipt tracking does not replace evidence submission or IPO approval. Existing sample quotations are not retroactively given approval/receipt timestamps.

Owner targets: survey receipt to prepared saved quotation 15 minutes excluding recorded costing-review waiting; management quotation submission to decision 20 minutes; recorded PO receipt to IPO submission 15 minutes; saved IPO to Operations handoff 30 minutes. Aging is displayed in minutes/hours/days. The system tracks continuous elapsed time, not inferred keyboard activity or working hours. Target performance denominators contain only recorded completed transitions. Activity counts use the last 30 days; turnaround ratios currently use all recorded measurable completions. Customer waiting and management waiting are distinct responsibilities.

A sample under Atif Hameed's Coordinator login uses Demo Sites and is named SAMPLE – Atif Dashboard Training. It is a survey draft for practicing bucket progression, not a real sale.

Management defaults to Team work, with an optional filter for documents awaiting management action. Opening a pending costing task opens its exact review, including submissions owned by a Coordinator. Stage cards show overdue counts. Sample documents carry a Training sample label.

The explicitly requested `atif-training-20-v1` batch provides two proposals in each of ten stages, with fictional customers prefixed SAMPLE and initial ages spanning minutes through days. It uses the normal repositories, validation and approval rules inside one atomic transaction. Initial timestamps are synthetic and set only at insertion; historical snapshots are never updated. Performance figures currently include training records, as stated on the dashboard. One batch registry makes repeat execution idempotent; no automatic deployment seeding or public seed endpoint exists.

Operator command after applying migrations: `pnpm exec tsx scripts/dashboard-samples.ts`, with `DATABASE_URL` or a private `SAMPLE_DATABASE_FILE`, and `DATABASE_SSL=true` for an external TLS connection. On the hosted service, explicitly setting `PZ_DASHBOARD_SAMPLE_BATCH=atif-training-20-v1` runs this same batch once on startup using its existing private database connection; clear the setting after verifying the batch. Without this exact operator setting, startup creates no samples. The batch requires the existing active `bess@powerzone.com.pk` Coordinator and an active management reviewer. Customer database imports and existing customer IDs are preserved; sample customers use fresh IDs. Remove the training batch only as part of the owner's separately planned sample-data cleanup.
